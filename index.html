Nimba Security · Cybersecurity Services

Secure your people
before attackers do.

As SA businesses move ERP and HR systems to the cloud, the attack surface grows. Nimba Security closes that gap through training, simulation and rigorous testing — bundled with every Nimba People deployment.

Security Awareness Training
Core Service
Hands-on training covering social engineering, phishing, BEC fraud and SA-specific threats like SARS scams, WhatsApp OTP hijacking and fake supplier banking details.
Penetration Testing
Pre-Handover
Full pentest before go-live. We find vulnerabilities, exploit them safely, fix critical issues and issue a signed remediation certificate before handover.
Phishing Simulation
Assessment
Simulated phishing campaigns sent to your staff — click rates reported by department to drive targeted, no-blame awareness training.
Ongoing Monitoring
Retainer
Post-deployment monitoring, quarterly training refreshers, patch guidance and incident response support for continued protection.
Our Process
The 5-Phase Pentest
01
Reconnaissance
Map attack surface, exposed subdomains, services and employee data available online.
02
Scanning
Vulnerability scanning of all internet-facing ports, APIs and authentication endpoints.
03
Exploitation
Controlled exploitation — credential attacks, injection vulnerabilities, session hijacking.
04
Social Engineering Test
Simulated phishing sent to staff. Click rate measured by department. No blame, only awareness.
05
Report & Remediate
Full findings report. Critical issues fixed. Signed certificate issued before handover.

No ERP goes live without a pentest.

Every Nimba People deployment includes a Nimba Security assessment. We attack your system before criminals do — and fix what we find.
